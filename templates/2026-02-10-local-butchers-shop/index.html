Our Story

A Cut Above the Rest

At PLACEHOLDER BUTCHERS, we believe that great meals start with great ingredients. For over 35 years, our family has been serving the PLACEHOLDER community with the finest cuts of meat, sourced from local farms that share our commitment to quality and ethical practices.

Every cut is prepared by our expert butchers who bring decades of experience and a genuine passion for their craft. From the perfect steak to specialty sausages made from our secret recipes, we take pride in everything that leaves our shop.
