Welcome In

More Than Just a Pub

At PLACEHOLDER PUB, we've been bringing the community together for over 25 years. We're your local spot for a cheeky pint after work, a hearty Sunday roast with the family, or a night out with friends watching the big game.

Our bistro kitchen serves up classic pub favourites with a modern twist, using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients. From our famous burgers to our perfectly poured pints, everything is made with care and served with a smile.
